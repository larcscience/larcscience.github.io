Getting to UC Riverside

There are two convenient airports: Ontario International (ONT), which is closest, and Los Angeles International (LAX), which has more flights but is much further away. Fares and times below are approximate and vary with traffic, time of day and surge pricing; check the Uber app or the Metrolink website for current prices and schedules.

From Ontario International Airport (ONT)

ONT is about 20 miles northwest of UCR. There is no convenient public transit connection, so please take an Uber (or Lyft) from ONT directly to campus.

  • Uber fare: roughly $45–$65 for UberX
  • Travel time: about 30 minutes

From Los Angeles International Airport (LAX)

LAX is about 70 miles west of Riverside. There are two options:

Option 1: Uber

  • Uber fare: roughly $115–$180 for UberX
  • Travel time: 90 minutes or more; LA traffic can make this considerably longer at rush hour

Option 2: FlyAway bus + Metrolink train

Total cost is about $25 and the journey takes roughly 2.5–3 hours including connections.

  1. LAX → LA Union Station: take the LAX FlyAway bus to Union Station. It stops at every terminal, runs every 30 minutes at peak times, takes about 45–60 minutes, and costs about $12.75 (no cash on board; buy online or at the kiosk).
  2. LA Union Station → Riverside–Downtown: take a Metrolink train on either the Riverside Line or the 91/Perris Valley Line. Both take about 1.5 hours and cost roughly $10–$14 one way. Under the current (May 2026) weekday timetable, trains leave Union Station for Riverside–Downtown at approximately:
    • 91/Perris Valley Line: 7:33am, 9:33am, 12:33pm, 3:33pm, 4:33pm, 5:33pm
    • Riverside Line: 10:35am, 3:35pm, 4:35pm, 5:35pm, 6:35pm
    The last train is in the early evening, so if you land late, take an Uber instead. Schedules may change before January 2027; please check before you travel.
  3. Riverside–Downtown station → UCR: the campus is about 4 miles away. A short Uber (about 10–15 minutes) is the easiest option.
